### 3.4 Important experimental caveat (from SwinIR paper)

> "Since there is no ground-truth high-quality images, we only provide visual comparison with representative bicubic model ESRGAN and state-of-the-art real-world image SR models RealSR, BSRGAN and Real-ESRGAN."

Meaning: original SwinIR paper does **not** publish PSNR/SSIM on the RealSR paired benchmark; quantitative RealSR numbers for SwinIR come from later papers (e.g., HAT extended) that evaluate official checkpoints.
