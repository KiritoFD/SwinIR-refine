# F2 — Model capacity vs data regime for real-world / RealSR-style SR

Access date: **2026-09-10**  
Scope: param↔PSNR trade-offs on real-degradation SR; diminishing returns with limited real pairs (~hundreds); typical param budgets (5M / 12M / 20M+).  
Local context (from `brief.md`): RealSR V3 x2, ~406 train pairs, 8GB laptop GPU; matrix compares ~0.6M → ~12M.

Confidence key: **high** = numbers stated in paper tables/text; **medium** = derived/indirect; **low** = weak inference.

---

## 1) How model size trades off with PSNR on real-world / real-degradation SR

### Claim 1.1 — On RealSR (hundreds of authentic pairs), a huge PSNR-oriented CNN buys only a small gain over mid-size nets; architecture/inductive bias dominates raw depth.
- **Evidence:** RealSR paper trains VDSR, SRResNet, RCAN (>400 conv layers) on RealSR pairs. RCAN improves only **~0.2–0.3 dB** over VDSR/SRResNet. LP-KPN (46 conv layers, **&lt;1/5 of RCAN parameters**) achieves the **best** PSNR/SSIM among all competitors on the RealSR test set (Tables 2–3, 6).
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00523 (HTML: https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523)
- **Date:** 2019-04-01 (ICCV 2019)
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 1.2 — Larger capacity on limited real pairs increases overfitting risk and can hurt cross-domain generalization.
- **Evidence:** RealSR cross-camera section: “Between RCAN and LP-KPN models, the former has **more parameters and thus is easier to overfit** to the training set, delivering slightly worse generalization capability than LP-KPN.” Cross-camera gap is small (~0.3 dB) for both, but LP-KPN generalizes at least as well with far fewer params.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523 (Sec. 5.3)
- **Date:** 2019-04-01
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 1.3 — Doubling SwinIR capacity yields sub-0.1 dB PSNR; better blocks beat raw size.
- **Evidence:** HAT complexity tables (bicubic ×4 Urban100, fair setup, no pretrain):  
  - SwinIR **11.9M → 27.45 dB**  
  - SwinIR-L1 **24.0M → 27.53 dB** (+0.08 dB for ~2× params)  
  - SwinIR-L2 **23.1M → 27.58 dB**  
  - HAT **20.8M → 27.97 dB** (+0.4–0.5 dB at similar budget via architecture)  
  - HAT-S **9.6M → 27.80 dB** (beats 24M SwinIR-L with fewer params)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04437 (Appendix B, Table 11)
- **Date:** 2022-05-09 / v3 2023-03-19
- **Confidence:** high (bicubic metrics, not RealSR; used as capacity-scaling evidence)

### Claim 1.4 — SwinIR ablations: PSNR rises with width/depth, but depth gains saturate; width grows params quadratically.
- **Evidence:** “PSNR is positively correlated with [channel number, RSTB number, STL number]. For channel number, although the performance keeps increasing, the total number of parameters grows **quadratically**. … As for RSTB number and layer number, the **performance gain becomes saturated gradually**.” Chosen defaults: C=180, 6 RSTB × 6 STL → **~11.8–11.9M**.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2108.10257 (Sec. 4.2)
- **Date:** 2021-08-23
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 1.5 — For real-world SR, degradation modeling / data strategy dominates architecture choice among challenge entrants.
- **Evidence:** NTIRE 2020 Real-World SR report: “the results in the challenge suggest that **training strategy and careful degradation modelling is far more important than choice of SR architecture**. For the latter, most top methods simply adopted popular architectures, such as the RRDB/ESRGAN and the RCAN.”
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01996
- **Date:** 2020-05-05
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 1.6 — Perception-oriented real-world generators (ESRGAN/Real-ESRGAN/BSRGAN) sit in the ~12–17M band, not 50M+.
- **Evidence:** Real-ESRGAN reuses the ESRGAN RRDB generator (23 RRDB, typically **~16.7M**). BSRGAN also adopts ESRGAN. SwinIR real-world models use the **~11.8M** middle config; a larger SwinIR-L is trained only with **much larger multi-source datasets**. ESRGAN paper: deeper RRDB + more data improves perceptual quality, not a param-vs-PSNR study on RealSR.
- **URLs:**  
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833 (Real-ESRGAN)  
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14006 (BSRGAN)  
  - https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00219 (ESRGAN)  
  - https://github.com/JingyunLiang/SwinIR
- **Date:** 2018–2021
- **Confidence:** high for “~12–17M is the common generator band”; medium for exact 16.7M (widely cited RRDB count, not always restated in Real-ESRGAN text)

## 2) When larger models stop helping with limited training pairs (~hundreds)

### Claim 2.1 — RealSR-scale data (~100–400 pairs/camera/scale) is the regime where big PSNR nets overfit and gains flatten.
- **Evidence:** RealSR V1: **86–117 pairs per camera × scale** (Canon/Nikon ×2/×3/×4); V3: **459 train / 100 test scenes** (~400 pairs for x2 Canon+Nikon in the local brief). On this data, RCAN’s depth advantage is only 0.2–0.3 dB and overfits more than LP-KPN. Paper design choice: efficient kernel prediction rather than more layers.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00523 ; https://github.com/csjcai/RealSR
- **Date:** 2019
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 2.2 — Transformers that need large pretraining do not help (or fail) without thousands–millions of images.
- **Evidence:**  
  - IPT: **115.5M params** and **&gt;1.1M ImageNet images** required for good performance (SwinIR discussion).  
  - HAT: “We use DF2K … since we find that using **only DIV2K will lead to overfitting**.” Same-task ImageNet pretraining (1.28M images, 800K iters) is introduced to unlock scale; large models need large data + careful fine-tune LR.  
  - SwinIR counterpoint: still beats CNN baselines at **25% DIV2K (200 images)**, so *well-regularized* mid-size transformers can work in low-data, but 20M+ without pretrain is risky.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/html/2108.10257 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2205.04437
- **Date:** 2021–2023
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 2.3 — Practical “stop helping” thresholds from published capacity ablations.
- **Evidence synthesis (not a single paper’s RealSR sweep):**  
  - Depth (RSTB/STL): gains **saturate around 6×6** for SwinIR-class models on DIV2K (SwinIR).  
  - Width: params ∝ C²; PSNR still climbs but cost explodes (SwinIR).  
  - At ~20–24M vs ~12M: **+0.05–0.13 dB** typical on classical SR when architecture is fixed (HAT Table 11).  
  - On RealSR pairs, **~3–5M inductive-bias models (LP-KPN) match or beat 15M+ RCAN** (RealSR).  
  - **Implication for ~400 RealSR x2 pairs:** expect diminishing/negative returns above roughly **~5–12M** unless strong augmentation, synthetic degradation mix, or external pretraining is used. 20M+ is justified mainly when training on synthetic large sets (BSRGAN/Real-ESRGAN/SwinIR-L recipe), not on hundreds of authentic pairs alone.
- **URLs:** as above
- **Date:** 2026-09-10 (synthesis)
- **Confidence:** medium–high (high for component numbers; medium for the numeric cutoff extrapolated to RealSR-only training)

### Claim 2.4 — More *diverse* data, not more params, is the lever papers actually scale for real-world quality.
- **Evidence:** ESRGAN: DIV2K+Flickr2K+OST improves textures; larger patch helps deep nets. Real-ESRGAN/BSRGAN: train on synthetic high-order degradations over DF2K+OST (thousands of images), not on RealSR’s hundreds. SwinIR real-world: DIV2K+Flickr2K+OST for M; add WED+FFHQ+Manga109+SCUT-CTW for L.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00219 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833 ; https://github.com/JingyunLiang/SwinIR
- **Date:** 2018–2021
- **Confidence:** high

| **~0.5–1M** | SwinIR-light (**0.878M**); lightweight challenge methods | SwinIR Table 3; NTIRE teams |

| **~5–6M** | Mid compact residual nets used as baselines; local matrix “largeish” band | local brief + practice |

### Claim 3.1 — On authentic RealSR pairs only, published success used **small-to-mid** models (LP-KPN ~3M; RCAN ~15.6M with modest extra gain).
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00523
- **Date:** 2019
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 3.2 — Modern real-world *visual* SR (BSRGAN / Real-ESRGAN / SwinIR-real) standardizes on **~12M** (SwinIR-M) or **~16–17M** (RRDB), with **20M+ only when data is synthetic-large**.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14006 ; https://github.com/JingyunLiang/SwinIR
- **Date:** 2021
- **Confidence:** high

### Claim 3.3 — For **~400 RealSR V3 x2 pairs, 8GB GPU**, literature supports prioritizing **~4–12M** over 20M+.
- **Rationale:** matches LP-KPN/SwinIR-M budgets; avoids IPT/HAT-scale data requirements; HAT/SwinIR show ~2× params → &lt;0.1 dB; RealSR shows overfitting of larger nets on limited pairs.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.00523 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2108.10257 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04437
- **Date:** 2026-09-10 (synthesis)
- **Confidence:** medium–high

## Direct answers to the three research questions

1. **Param ↔ PSNR (real-degradation):** monotone but weakly so. On RealSR, RCAN vs smaller nets: **+0.2–0.3 dB**. On fixed-architecture transformer scaling: **~2× params ≈ +0.05–0.13 dB**. Architecture (LP-KPN, HAT blocks) &gt; raw params.  
2. **Diminishing returns at ~hundreds of pairs:** effectively after **~5–12M** without external data; RealSR explicitly notes larger models overfit; HAT needs ImageNet-scale pretrain for 20M+; IPT needs 115M + 1.1M images.  
3. **Typical budgets:** **~3–5M** (efficient RealSR-native), **~12M** (SwinIR-M real-world), **~16–17M** (ESRGAN/Real-ESRGAN), **20M+** (HAT / large SwinIR with synthetic or pretrain data). **5M vs 12M** is a reasonable local ablation axis; **20M+** is poorly justified on ~400 authentic pairs alone.

## Caveats

- HAT Table 11 and most SwinIR param curves are **bicubic/classical SR**, not RealSR PSNR. Used for *capacity scaling shape*, not absolute RealSR numbers.  
- RealSR PSNR is on Y channel after RGB training (V3 protocol); visual/perceptual metrics are separate.  
- Exact RRDB param count (~16.7M) is community-standard; Real-ESRGAN paper does not restate it in the abstract/tables fetched.  
- No published sweep trains the *same* SwinIR at 5/12/20M on RealSR-only pairs; the 5/12/20 guidance is a literature-backed extrapolation.
