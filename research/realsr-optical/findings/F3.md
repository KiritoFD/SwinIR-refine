# F3 — What actually raises PSNR/SSIM on RealSR (or equivalent real-camera SR)

Access date: **2026-09-13**.  
Scope: 2019–2025 papers with claim-backed numbers on **RealSR (Cai et al.)** or closely equivalent *paired real-camera* benchmarks (DPED-style only when noted). DIV2K-only gains are excluded from “what works on RealSR.”  
Metric note: official RealSR protocol is **Y-channel PSNR/SSIM** (RGB train / Y test on V3). V1 tables below are also Y-channel.

## Executive summary (3–5 lines)

1. **Data authenticity is the dominant RealSR PSNR lever**: training on RealSR pairs vs DIV2K bicubic (BD) or DIV2K multi-degradation (MD) is worth **~0.5–1.0 dB at ×2** on the same backbone; BD→MD is almost free (~0.01–0.03 dB).  
2. **Spatially-variant restoration (per-pixel kernels / LP-KPN)** beats matched-capacity direct pixel synthesis, and large kernels / Laplacian pyramid give consistent small ablation gains.  
3. **Capacity helps but is second-order** on RealSR (~0.2–0.3 dB RCAN over VDSR); progressive **multi-scale alignment** is a prerequisite, not a free bonus.  
4. **GAN / perception-oriented degradation recipes (DSGAN, BSRGAN, Real-ESRGAN, NTIRE winners)** win MOS/NIQE, **not** RealSR PSNR—do not expect +dB from them.  
5. **Not worth doing for RealSR PSNR**: DIV2K-only architecture scaling, GAN fine-tunes, or “more complex synthetic blur+noise” without real-pair transfer evidence.

## 2. What *is* worth doing (for RealSR PSNR/SSIM)

1. **Use authentic (or best-available camera-matched) LR–HR pairs** — largest single ablation-backed jump.  
2. **Invest in registration quality** (RealSR: progressive coarse→central crop→fine affine + luminance; discard misaligned pairs). Without this, supervised PSNR is ill-posed.  
3. **Model spatially varying kernels** if you stay small (KPN/LP-KPN). Gains are real but **~0.1–0.2 dB** class—far smaller than data.  
4. **Prefer L1/L2 (or Charbonnier) for the PSNR track**. RealSR PSNR models are L2-trained; perception models are a different operating point.  
5. **Expect modest capacity scaling** (deep residual / attention) only after data is matched; RealSR paper shows RCAN’s deep capacity is second-order vs data.

## 3. What is *not* worth doing (for RealSR PSNR)

| Idea | Why not (for PSNR) | Evidence |
|------|--------------------|----------|
| DIV2K **bicubic-only** training for real camera inputs | Stays ~bicubic on RealSR (VDSR/SRResNet + BD ≈ bicubic) | RealSR Table 2 |
| **MD simulated degradations** as a PSNR strategy | +0.01–0.03 dB only on RealSR | RealSR Table 2 |
| **GAN / ESRGAN-style** fine-tunes as a PSNR strategy | Designed for MOS/NIQE; known perception–distortion tradeoff | DSGAN, Real-ESRGAN, NTIRE 2019–20 winners |
| **BSRGAN / Real-ESRGAN high-order synthetic** claimed as RealSR *PSNR* wins | Papers sell **visual** real-world quality, not paired Y-PSNR on RealSR | arXiv:2103.14006, 2107.10833 |
| **DIV2K architecture SOTA** (HAT, SwinIR, etc.) without real-data transfer study | +1 dB on bicubic DIV2K ≠ RealSR | HAT arXiv:2205.04437 (explicitly DIV2K) |
| Chasing **kernel size** beyond ~19 / beyond LP-KPN | Diminishing returns after LP-KPN | RealSR Table 3 |
| **Test-time ensembles / TTA** as a “method” | Often challenge-only; not a mechanism; small, not re-ablated on RealSR Y | NTIRE reports |

## 4. Protocol warnings (do not mix tables)

- **RealSR V1** (paper): 30 test scenes/camera × scale; Y-channel; numbers in Table 2/3 (~32.6 bicubic ×2).  
- **RealSR V2** (GitHub): 100 test scenes; LP-KPN ×2 **33.49 / 0.917**.  
- **RealSR V3**: RGB train / Y test; official baseline only for ×3/×4 (no ×2 row in README).  
- **NTIRE 2019** used a RealSR *challenge* subset; results are often perceptual (MOS/NIQE), not comparable to V1 Y-PSNR.  
- **DRealSR / AIM / NTIRE 2020 DPED** are different real-camera sets—useful for transfer claims, **not** for “RealSR ×2 PSNR SOTA.”

## 6. Bottom line for an optical / RealSR engineering track

If the goal is **higher RealSR Y-PSNR/SSIM**, prioritize in this order:

1. **Better paired real data + alignment** (camera, focal ladder, registration, pair filtering).  
2. **L2/L1-trained regressor** with moderate depth (RCAN-class is enough).  
3. **Optional:** spatially-variant kernel heads (LP-KPN) if parameter budget is tight.  
4. **Do not** expect BSRGAN/Real-ESRGAN/GAN stacks or DIV2K SOTA transformers to move RealSR PSNR without a dedicated real-pair training and ablation.
