# F3 — Physics-prior methods that survive few-hundred real pairs

**Date:** 2026-09-13  
**Question:** Which physics-prior families still work on RealSR-scale data (~400 authentic pairs), and why does a small kernel-head beat a deep pixel synthesizer (RCAN)?  
**Local context:** RealSR V3 ×2, ~406 train pairs, 8GB GPU; parent brief asks for *one or two theoretically forced* next moves, not more module ablations.  
**Confidence key:** High = stated in paper tables/text; Medium = derived/synthesis; Low = extrapolation.

Related prior findings: `research/realsr-param-matrix/findings/F2.md` (capacity vs data), `F3.md` (RealSR protocol).

---

## 1) RealSR LP-KPN: why small + kernel head won

### Claim 1.1 — Real degradations are spatially variant (depth/DoF), so a *global* inverse is the wrong inductive bias.
- **Evidence:** RealSR paper: blur kernel “varies with the depth in a scene” and DoF changes with focal length; degradation is explicitly called **spatially variant**. Direct pixel synthesis “may not be the cost-effective way.”
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1 (Sec. 4)  
- **Date:** 2019-04-01 (ICCV 2019)  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 1.2 — KPN outputs a *per-pixel local linear operator* (not an arbitrary HR image); this is a physics prior, not a bigger net.
- **Evidence:** HR prediction is  
  `I_H^P(i,j) = ⟨K(i,j), V(I_L^A(i,j))⟩`  
  i.e. a weighted linear combination of a **k×k neighborhood** of the *input* LR. Output lives in the span of local LR patches — a hard constraint that cannot invent arbitrary high-frequency phase.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1 (Eq. 8)  
- **Date:** 2019-04-01  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 1.3 — LP-KPN buys a large effective RF with tiny kernels: three 5×5 Laplacian-level kernels ≈ one 19×19 RF.
- **Evidence:** Three-level Laplacian pyramid; learning three k×k kernels “can equally lead to a receptive field with size **4k×4k** at the original resolution.” LP-KPN (three 5×5) **beats** single-scale KPN with 19×19 at far lower cost. Backbones: **46 conv layers**, **<1/5 of RCAN params**, ~**3× faster** than RCAN.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1 (Sec. 4, Sec. 7.4)  
- **Date:** 2019-04-01  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 1.4 — On RealSR, kernel-head + small backbone beats deep pixel synthesis (RCAN).
| Method (RealSR-trained, Y) | ×2 | ×3 | ×4 | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| VDSR / SRResNet | ~33.6 | ~30.1 | ~28.6 | mid-depth direct synthesis |
| **RCAN** (>400 layers, ~15.6M) | **33.87 / 0.922** | 30.40 | 28.88 | best direct synthesis |
| KPN (k=19) | 33.86 / 0.924 | 30.39 | 28.90 | matches RCAN |
| **LP-KPN (k=5, <1/5 RCAN)** | **33.90 / 0.927** | **30.42** | **28.92** | **best + lowest cost** |

- **Evidence:** Paper Tables (V1, Y-channel); official V2 README: KPN k=19 33.45 / LP-KPN k=5 **33.49** (×2). RCAN’s depth buys only **~0.2–0.3 dB** over VDSR and loses to LP-KPN.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1 ; https://github.com/csjcai/RealSR  
- **Date:** 2019 / accessed 2026-09-13  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 1.5 — Why this is a *low-data* win (mechanism, not just number).
1. **Hard output constraint:** KPN can only reweight existing LR neighborhoods → less memorization capacity than an unrestricted residual mapper.  
2. **Spatially adaptive without free parameters per pixel:** kernel tensor is predicted from a shared trunk; adaptation is structured.  
3. **Laplacian multi-scale** = inductive bias matching optical scale-space (blur ~ low-pass; details at finer levels).  
4. **Fewer params** (<1/5 RCAN) → less overfitting on ~100–400 pairs per camera×scale.  
- **Evidence synthesis** from Claims 1.1–1.4 + cross-camera overfitting note (Claim 4.1).  
- **Confidence:** High for the paper facts; Medium–High for the mechanism synthesis.

## 2) Blind / degradation-aware SR that still works with few hundred pairs

### Claim 2.1 — Two distinct strategies; only one is data-cheap.
| Family | Needs huge synthetic DF2K-style set? | Works on RealSR-scale pairs? |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Internal / test-time kernel priors** (KernelGAN + ZSSR) | **No** (per-image) | Yes — zero pairs needed for kernel |
| **B. Explicit kernel-conditioned SR** (IKC/SFTMD) | Yes for training (DIV2K+Flickr2K, ~3450 imgs) | Weak unless fine-tuned carefully |
| **C. High-order synthetic degradation** (BSRGAN, Real-ESRGAN) | **Yes** (DF2K+OST, 1–2M iters) | Visual OK; PSNR on RealSR pairs often worse than small supervised |

- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06581 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03377 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14006 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833  
- **Dates:** 2019–2021  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 2.2 — KernelGAN: image-specific internal GAN recovers the true downscale kernel without *any* training set.
- **Evidence:** Trains solely on the **LR test image**; generator learns a downsampling op such that its patch distribution matches the LR (cross-scale recurrence). Fully unsupervised; plug into ZSSR / any SR. Prior work: true SR-kernel **maximizes patch recurrence across scales**.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06581 ; code https://github.com/sefibk/KernelGAN  
- **Date:** NeurIPS 2019 (oral)  
- **Confidence:** High  
- **Relevance to RealSR:** If residual blur on our aligned pairs is near-uniform *per image* (or per patch), KernelGAN-style estimates can **separate** kernel from texture without learning from 400 pairs. Spatially variant case needs **patch-wise** KernelGAN (costly but physics-correct).

### Claim 2.3 — IKC: one-shot kernel estimation is biased; *iterate* using artifact feedback.
- **Evidence:** Kernel mismatch produces **regular artifacts** (over-sharpen / over-smooth). IKC alternates predictor → corrector + SFTMD (spatial feature transform conditioned on kernel map). Better than direct kernel estimation.
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03377 ; code https://github.com/yuanjunchai/IKC  
- **Date:** CVPR 2019  
- **Confidence:** High  
- **Failure note:** IKC/SFTMD training uses **DF2K+Flickr2K (3450 images)** with synthetic Gaussian kernels. On RealSR, a *global* Gaussian prior is misspecified (spatially variant + non-Gaussian). Using IKC’s “iterate to correct kernel” idea with a **small KPN head** is the data-cheap hybrid.

### Claim 2.4 — BSRGAN / Real-ESRGAN win *visual* quality via synthetic diversity, not by being better on RealSR *pairs*.
- **Evidence:** BSRGAN: shuffled blur↓noise + JPEG/ISP; Real-ESRGAN: **high-order** (second round of blur↓noise) + U-Net discriminator. Both train on **DF2K+OST** (thousands of images), not RealSR’s hundreds. NTIRE 2020 real-world SR: “**training strategy and careful degradation modelling is far more important than choice of SR architecture**.”
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14006 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10833 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01996  
- **Dates:** 2020–2021  
- **Confidence:** High  
- **Implication for us:** If the goal is **PSNR on RealSR V3**, synthetic-blind recipes are a *pretrain / regularizer*, not a replacement for small kernel-aware fine-tune on the 400 pairs.

### Claim 2.5 — What still holds at “few hundred pairs” (practical recipe family).
1. **Supervised small KPN/LP-KPN** on the pairs (proven).  
2. **Test-time internal kernel** (KernelGAN) + lightweight SR — data-free kernel stage.  
3. **Kernel-map conditioning with a *small* backbone** (SFTMD idea, 3–6M scale) rather than blind end-to-end 16M RRDB.  
4. Synthetic DF2K high-order models only as **init** + short fine-tune with **small LR**.  
- **Confidence:** Medium–High (synthesis; 1 is paper-backed; 2–4 are extrapolations consistent with F2 capacity findings).

## 3) Separating alignment from restoration

### Claim 3.1 — RealSR itself separates them *offline*; residual misalignment remains a known issue.
- **Evidence:** Registration: PhotoShop lens-distortion correction → center crop → iterative **affine + luminance (α,β)** minimizing robust Lp; focal-ratio init; ~5 iters; **discard** misaligned pairs. Later works (BSRGAN discussion) note residual misalignment vs synthetic pairs.
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1#S3.SS3 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14006  
- **Date:** 2019 / 2021  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 3.2 — Why L1/L2 then *hurt*: pixelwise losses assume a **fixed phase correspondence** that RealSR does not fully provide.
- **Evidence (mechanistic):** Under residual sub-pixel shift τ, `||I_pred − I_H||` mixes restoration error with **alignment error**. High frequencies are phase-sensitive: a 0.5px shift can dominate HF loss while being invisible in coarse structure. This is the mathematical reason residual-bicubic + L1 plateaus and why “more loss terms” (HF, UWCL) can add noise rather than signal (see F4 track).
- **URL (contextual, phase-robust losses):** Contextual / correspondence losses and Fourier-domain adaptation literature are the standard fix family; RealSR paper does not study them.  
- **Confidence:** Medium (mechanism High; no single RealSR paper table)

### Claim 3.3 — Three *principled* de-align-or-ignore designs (ordered by fit to our setup).
| Design | Idea | Fit to ~400 pairs + 8GB |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Explicit warp head** | Predict local affine/flow, warp LR to HR grid, then restore | Extra params; can co-adapt badly; needs careful detaching |
| **B. Phase/shift-insensitive loss** | Match **magnitude** spectra, patch CX, or SSD on low-pass only | Cheap; reduces identifiability conflict |
| **C. Alignment-aware kernel (recommended hybrid)** | Let the **KPN kernel absorb small residual shift** (a shifted kernel is still a local linear filter) | Zero new modules; already the LP-KPN form |

- **Rationale for C:** A per-pixel k×k kernel **can represent a sub-pixel shift** (delta at fractional index). Thus residual affine misalignment is *already* inside the KPN hypothesis class — but only if the loss does not force the network to also “undo” phase with unconstrained pixel synthesis.  
- **Confidence:** Medium–High (math of C is solid; empirical validation needed)

### Claim 3.4 — Practical phase-safe supervision (minimal change to v2).
1. **Low-pass / Laplacian-level L1** (coarse levels of the same pyramid LP-KPN already uses) — ignore finest phase.  
2. **Kernel-applied supervision:** compare `K_pred * I_L` vs `I_H` *after* optional 1–2px local search or on low-pass.  
3. Optional: **Fourier amplitude loss** (phase-free) + small L1 weight.  
- **Avoid:** UWCL / pure CX without pixel term (our E3: 29.69 vs SwinIR-L1 30.54 — perceptual terms without alignment model lost PSNR).  
- **Local evidence:** `realsr-param-matrix/REPORT.md` E1–E5.  
- **Confidence:** Medium

## 4) Failure modes: overfit kernels, circular estimates, why RCAN lost

### Claim 4.1 — RCAN’s failure on RealSR is **overfitting**, not under-capacity.
- **Evidence (direct quote, Sec. 5.3):** “Between RCAN and LP-KPN models, the former has **more parameters and thus is easier to overfit** to the training set, delivering slightly worse generalization capability than LP-KPN.” Cross-camera gaps: RCAN **0.32 dB**, LP-KPN **0.30 dB** (both small; LP-KPN better + 5× smaller).
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1#S5.SS3  
- **Date:** 2019-04-01  
- **Confidence:** High

### Claim 4.2 — Local replication of “more params failed.”
- **Evidence:** On 406 RealSR ×2 pairs, 4k steps: SwinIR-light 0.61M → **30.43**; SwinIR-largeish 3.96M → **30.54** (+0.11); SwinIR-classical 11.75M @batch1 → **28.73** (under-trained); Mod-large 10.2M @batch1 → collapse. Architecture/optimization dominate raw width.
- **URL:** `research/realsr-param-matrix/REPORT.md`  
- **Date:** 2026-09-10  
- **Confidence:** High (local); note batch/sample confound for E5/E4.

### Claim 4.3 — Overfitting *kernels* specifically (KPN failure mode).
| Failure | Mechanism | Symptom |
|---|---|---|
| **Kernel memorization** | Large k / unconstrained k×k head learns scene-specific filters | Train PSNR up, cross-camera/test flat; kernels look like edge detectors, not blur |
| **Phase-absorbing kernels** | Network uses kernel to encode shift+texture jointly without low-pass constraint | Halo / ringing; poor HF stability |
| **Too-small k / no pyramid** | RF cannot cover real blur support | Over-smooth; underfits depth-variant blur |
- **Mitigation from LP-KPN:** Laplacian levels + modest k=5 + shared trunk + L2 on reconstructed pyramid.  
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1  
- **Confidence:** High for LP-KPN design intent; Medium for the failure taxonomy (synthesis).

### Claim 4.4 — Circular / non-identifiable kernel estimates.
| Cycle | What goes wrong |
|---|---|
| **KernelGAN internal recurrence** | If the LR has **weak multi-scale recurrence** (heavy noise, JPEG, non-self-similar texture), estimated kernel collapses to near-delta or over-blurs. |
| **IKC single-pass** | Predictor is biased; without corrector loop you get consistent *wrong* kernels (over-sharp/over-smooth). |
| **Joint end-to-end kernel+SR** | Degeneracy: SR can “explain” the kernel and vice versa; no unique (K, I_H) without priors (non-convex bilinear `K * I_H ≈ I_L`). |
| **Synthetic-kernel pretrain → real pairs** | Gaussian-σ mismatch: RealSR kernels are **not** isotropic Gaussians (lens + sensor + ISP). Fine-tune on 400 pairs is required; otherwise circular “my kernel is whatever the synthetic model said.” |
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.06581 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03377  
- **Confidence:** High for IKC/KernelGAN statements; Medium–High for identifiability argument.

### Claim 4.5 — Why “add params” is the wrong axis on RealSR (summary).
1. **True bottleneck is the operator model** (spatially variant blur + residual warp), not texture capacity.  
2. **~400 pairs** cannot identify 15M free parameters of an unconstrained mapper; they *can* identify a **shared small trunk + local linear kernels**.  
3. Extra depth without a physics constraint **fits residual misalignment** as if it were texture → looks good in train PSNR, fails cross-camera / real sensors.  
4. Literature agrees: NTIRE 2020 — degradation modelling > architecture; HAT/SwinIR — 2× params ≈ +0.05–0.13 dB on *synthetic* bicubic, worse regime on real pairs.  
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01996 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04437 ; https://arxiv.org/html/1904.00523v1  
- **Confidence:** High

## 5) Implications for our next one-two moves (actionable)

| Priority | Move | Why forced by physics/data | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Keep capacity **4–6M** (not 12M+) on RealSR-only | F2 + RealSR overfit quote | — |
| **P1** | **Kernel head** (even a lightweight LP-KPN-style 5×5 × 3 levels) on top of / instead of pure pixel mapper | Spatially variant degradation is the true operator | moderate rework |
| **P1b** | **Low-pass / Laplacian-level L1** (drop or downweight finest-scale L1) | Separates alignment phase from restoration | loss-only |
| **P2** | Optional KernelGAN-style **per-image kernel diagnostic** (no training) on a few RealSR LR crops | Check whether a single global k is even meaningful per image | analysis only |
| **Avoid** | UWCL/perceptual-only; 16M RRDB from scratch on 400 pairs; HF loss without alignment model | Local E3 + NTIRE + RealSR evidence | — |

## Caveats

- RealSR V3 official README does **not** publish ×2 PSNR; ×2 numbers above are V1 paper / V2 README. Protocol (Y-channel, RGB train) matters for absolute dB.  
- KernelGAN assumes multi-scale patch recurrence — strong JPEG/noise breaks it; RealSR LR is relatively clean (DSLR), so more favorable than phone dumps.  
- Claim 3.3-C (kernels absorb residual shift) is theoretically sound but **not** ablated in the RealSR paper; treat as a testable hypothesis, not established fact.  
- BSRGAN/Real-ESRGAN “success” is primarily **perceptual**; do not expect them to beat LP-KPN-class models on RealSR pair PSNR without careful fine-tune.
